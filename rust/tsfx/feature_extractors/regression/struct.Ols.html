fn fit_prefixes(x: &[Vec<f64>], y: &[f64], min_cols: usize) -> Option<Vec<Self>>

Fit y against the first p columns of x, for every p from min_cols to all of them (sharing one QR decomposition, as the first p columns of a QR decomposition are one of the first p columns).

Each fit is statsmodels’ pseudoinverse solution: singular values below $10^{-15}$ times the largest are dropped, and the rank counts singular values above $\max(s) \cdot p \cdot \varepsilon$ (np.linalg.matrix_rank of the diagonal matrix of singular values). Like LAPACK’s SVD (which reduces a tall matrix by QR first), the Householder QR followed by a Jacobi SVD of the triangular factor resolves exactly collinear columns (a constant stretch of the series) to negligible singular values, so the minimum-norm solution is found.

A fit whose root mean squared residual is at most PERFECT_FIT_TOL times the scale of the terms (largest $|y_i| + \sum_j |X_{ij},\beta_j|$) is exact up to rounding, and its residual sum of squares is set to 0: the rounding noise would otherwise decide t-values and information criteria.

Well-conditioned fits that are far from exact take a faster path, see Ols::fit_prefixes_gram; its results agree with this one’s to within rounding, not bit for bit.

None if the design is not finite (numpy’s SVD raises LinAlgError); a non-finite y propagates into the results.

Source

fn fit_prefixes_gram( x: &[Vec<f64>], y: &[f64], min_cols: usize, ) -> Option<Vec<Self>>

Ols::fit_prefixes from the Gram matrix $X^T X$ and $X^T y$, built in one pass over the rows, and its Cholesky factor $R$ ($R^T R = X^T X$, the same $R$ as a QR decomposition up to signs). A Householder QR sweeps the whole design once per column, which on long series is limited by memory bandwidth; this reads it once.

The columns are scaled to unit norm first. Squaring the design squares its condition number, so this path is only taken when every fit is well conditioned (scaled condition number bound below MAX_GRAM_CONDITION: results within about $10^{6},\varepsilon$ relative of the QR path’s), leaves a residual large enough that its estimated relative error stays below MAX_GRAM_RESIDUAL_ERROR and is clearly not an exact fit. None otherwise, for the QR path.
