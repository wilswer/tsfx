adjusted_acovf 🔒
The adjusted autocovariances $\gamma(0), \dots, \gamma(l_{max})$ of the demeaned series, $\gamma(l) = \frac{1}{n - l} \sum_{t=1}^{n-l} (x_t - \mu)(x_{t+l} - \mu)$, as statsmodels’ acovf(x, adjusted=True) (direct sums; statsmodels switches to an FFT for long series, which differs only by rounding). max_lag must be below x.len().
lagged_mean 🔒
np.mean of term(i) for i in 0..n - 2 * lag, or 0 if the series is too short (n <= 2 * lag), as tsfresh.
levinson_durbin_pacf 🔒
Partial autocorrelations $\alpha_0, \dots, \alpha_m$ from the autocovariances $\gamma(0), \dots, \gamma(m)$ ($m \geq 1$) by the Levinson-Durbin recursion, exactly as statsmodels’ levinson_durbin.
