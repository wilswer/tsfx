Welch’s power spectral density estimate, as tsfresh calls scipy.signal.welch(x, nperseg=min(len(x), 256)): periodic Hann window, 50% overlap (noverlap = nperseg // 2), each segment’s mean removed, sampling frequency 1, density scaling, one-sided (bins other than DC and Nyquist doubled), averaged over segments.
